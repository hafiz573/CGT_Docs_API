Getting started ​

This site documents the Lua scripting surface of CreativeGrowtopia V1.50 - the functions your executor scripts can call. It is built for the libregrowtopia.so shipped in CreativeGrowtopia-V1.50-CPS.

The surface is namespaced: some functions are globals (GetTile, GetWorld, ...), and the rest live under tables you call as Namespace.method(...) - Client, Input, Render, Script, Proton, UI, Entities.

How this reference was produced ​

The authoritative list of names and their namespaces comes from a live in-game _G dump (108 names, hash 19601d21, identical on PC and Android). For each function we then disassemble the binary to recover:

  • parameters - from luaL_check* / luaL_opt* (and, for thunks, the argument reads in the prologue);
  • return-table fields - from each lua_setfield / lua_settable paired with the value pushed before it;
  • enum constants - from the integer literals pushed into a constants table.

Because the binary carries no prose, the descriptions here are inferred from the name and the recovered signature. They are guidance, not guarantees.

A first script ​

lua
-- Who am I, and where?
local me = GetLocal()
LogToConsole("I am " .. me.name .. " at " .. me.posX .. "," .. me.posY)

-- Read the tile I'm standing on
local t = GetTile(math.floor(me.posX / 32), math.floor(me.posY / 32))
LogToConsole("fg=" .. t.fg .. " bg=" .. t.bg .. " collidable=" .. tostring(t.collidable))

Tile/pixel conversions (the / 32 above) depend on the engine's tile size; adjust for your build.
